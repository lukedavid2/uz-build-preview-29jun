Rotated, the controls move to one top strip and the neck spans the full width — ~10 frets visible without scrolling, every CAGED position of Am7 readable at a glance. String gauges still thin (high e) → thick (low E); the root is white-ringed. This is where you'd actually work out a voicing.

Sticky orientation · The rotate button sets a persistent preference. If you leave the arpeggiator in landscape and jump back to the chord builder, then return, it reopens in landscape — and the builder itself honours the same orientation so the whole tool feels like one rotated surface, not two.
